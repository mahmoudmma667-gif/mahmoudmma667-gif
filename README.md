## Research Statement & Epistemological Philosophy | البيان البحثي والفلسفي

I am a **transdisciplinary researcher** operating at the systemic intersections of **Human-Computer Interaction (HCI)**, **Human-Centered Technology (HCT)**, **Science, Technology, and Society (STS)**, **Computer Accessibility**, **Educational Technology**, and **Complex Multiphysics Simulation**.

My research investigates the profound continuum between human intention, physiological signals, and computational execution. Rather than treating machines as isolated mathematical engines, my work conceptualizes computing systems as socio-technical substrates that must be fundamentally calibrated around human dignity, cognitive ergonomics, sensory diversity, and equitable access.

By bridging theoretical inquiry in Science and Technology Studies (STS) with rigorous empirical engineering across computer vision, neural interfaces, spatial mathematics, and thermodynamics, my mission is to pioneer frictionless, inclusive paradigms that dissolve technological alienation and empower all individuals regardless of physical or sensory constraints.

> **الرؤية البحثية والفلسفية:**  
> باحث ومبتكر علمي عابر للتخصصات، يستكشف فلسفة وتطبيقات **التكنولوجيا المتمحورة حول الإنسان (HCT)**، و**التفاعل بين الإنسان والحاسوب (HCI)**، ودراسات **العلوم والتكنولوجيا والمجتمع (STS)**.  
> ينطلق مشروعي البحثي من قناعة راسخة بأن النظم الحسابية والتقنية ليست غايات منعزلة، بل أدوات مجتمعية يجب أن تخضع لقيم الكرامة الإنسانية والشمول والعدالة المعرفية. أعمل على توظيف خوارزميات الذكاء الاصطناعي متعدد الأنماط، والرؤية الحاسوبية، والواجهات العصبية، والنمذجة الحسابية لمحاكاة النظم المعقدة، بهدف تطوير **نظم إمكانية الوصول الشامل (Computer Accessibility)**، و**بيئات تكنولوجيا التعليم التكيفية (Educational Technology)**، والحلول الطبية الحيوية المتقدمة التي تلغي الحواجز وتفتح آفاقاً جديدة للارتقاء بالإنسان.

### 1. Human-Computer Interaction (HCI) & Human-Centered Technology (HCT)
- **Natural & Perceptual User Interfaces (NUI/PUI)**: Markerless kinematic hand/body tracking, sub-pixel facial analysis, and multi-point gestural decomposition.
- **Spatial Computing & Contactless Interaction**: Translating biological gesture kinematics into watertight 3D Cartesian coordinates without specialized physical controllers.
- **RF-Sensing & Non-Optical Perception**: Fusing WiFi Channel State Information (CSI) with optical models for contactless human pose estimation, through-wall presence detection, and environmental spatial intelligence.
- **Multi-Modal Cognitive Symbiosis**: Synchronizing gaze, spatial gesture, and voice-first interaction into low-latency perceptual pipelines.

### 2. Science, Technology, and Society (STS) & Ethical Cybernetics
- **Epistemological Democratization**: Eliminating proprietary hardware dependencies by engineering robust interfaces on commodity, consumer-grade webcams and sensors.
- **Socio-Technical Governance of AI**: Analyzing algorithmic bias, automation displacement, cognitive offloading, and the societal repercussions of generative models on human knowledge architecture.
- **Human Agency Preservation**: Ensuring intelligent systems function as cognitive scaffolds that augment rather than erode human autonomy and decision sovereignty.

### 3. Computer Accessibility & Assistive Systems (إمكانية الوصول والتقنيات المساعدة)
- **Brain-Computer Interfaces (BCI)**: Translating non-invasive electroencephalographic (EEG) and electromyographic (EMG) signals into mobility and computing controls for motor-impaired and paralyzed users.
- **Multimodal Assistive Vision for the Visually Impaired**: Developing real-time scene understanding, obstacle localization, spatial audio sonification, and localized dialect dialogue systems (Aynak AI).
- **Universal Design & Barrier-Free Computing**: Implementing multi-modal sensory redundancy (tactile, visual, spatial audio) and adaptive thresholds conforming to modern digital equity mandates.

### 4. Educational Technology & Cognitive Pedagogy (تكنولوجيا التعليم والنظم التكيفية)
- **AI-Driven Adaptive Learning Architectures**: Dynamic student trajectory modeling, predictive mastery assessment, and personalized cognitive load balancing.
- **Spatial & Interactive STEM Pedagogy**: Transforming abstract mathematical, chemical, and physical concepts into tangible 3D interactive simulations.
- **Constructivist Epistemology in Digital Spaces**: Empowering learners to construct and manipulate knowledge artifacts directly within interactive virtual sandboxes.

### 5. Biomedical AI, Physiological Digital Twins & Radar Sensing
- **Multi-Scale Physiological Digital Twins**: Computational modeling of organ dynamics, physiological pharmacokinetics (PBPK), and VAE-driven anomalous biomarker detection (HUMIQ-X).
- **Non-Contact Radar Sensing (PULSAR)**: Combining Ultra-wideband (UWB) radar, FMCW mmWave radar, and Doppler RF with CNN-BiLSTM signal fusion for remote respiratory and cardiopulmonary diagnostics.
- **Deep Learning in Medical Imaging**: Advanced convolutional neural networks (CNNs) for high-accuracy lesion detection and multi-spectral MRI/CT volumetric segmentation.

### 6. Sustainable Energy Systems & Multiphysics Modeling
- **Carbon Capture & Electrochemical Conversion (CCU)**: Computational fluid dynamics (CFD) and thermodynamic optimization of electrochemical cells for CO2 conversion into sustainable fuels.
- **Thermoelectric Energy Harvesting (TEG)**: Modeling thermal gradients, Seebeck coefficients, and solid-state waste-heat recovery in industrial environments.
- **Finite Element Analysis (FEA) & Multiphysics**: Comprehensive simulation of heat transfer, fluid flow, and structural mechanics using COMSOL Multiphysics and SimScale.

## Selected Research Paradigms & Pioneered Concepts | نماذج بحثية ومفاهيم ريادية

- **Aynak AI — Assistive Multimodal Vision System**: A smartphone-based intelligent perceptual assistant for visually impaired individuals, integrating computer vision, spatial orientation, Arabic/Egyptian-dialect interaction, and contextual memory.
- **Flowx 3D OS — Contactless Spatial Interaction Platform**: A research-grade architecture for 3D modeling and spatial manipulation via camera-based hand kinematics and WiFi Channel State Information (CSI) fusion without proprietary controllers.
- **HUMIQ-X — Human Unified Medical Intelligence Quantum Exponential**: Large-scale biomedical AI simulation platform exploring organ digital twins (liver PBPK/zonal modeling), drug-induced liver injury (DILI) prediction, and VAE biomarker anomaly detection.
- **PULSAR — Pulmonary Ultra-wideband Sensing with AI & Radar**: Non-contact cardiopulmonary monitoring using UWB radar, FMCW mmWave radar, and Doppler RF with multimodal CNN-BiLSTM signal fusion for respiratory anomaly screening.
- **RF-Cortex — Dense Spatial RF Perception**: Exploiting WiFi CSI and ambient radio frequency perturbations for human pose estimation, motion segmentation, and unobtrusive environmental sensing.
- **EGY-HORUS.AI — Satellite Remote Sensing & Archaeology**: Deep learning pipelines utilizing Sentinel-1 SAR, Sentinel-2 optical, and ASTER multispectral satellite imagery for automated subterranean archaeological feature detection.
- **ARIA ZERO Touch — Hands-Free Computer Interaction**: Touchless desktop OS control engine utilizing real-time webcam gesture processing, OpenCV, MediaPipe, and PyQt5 for accessibility.
- **High-Efficiency Carbon Capture & Electrochemical Conversion Cell**: Multiphysics-modeled system coupling high-rate CO2 capture with electrochemical reduction to produce sustainable methanol.
- **Thermoelectric Waste-Heat Recovery Generator (TEG)**: Industrial solid-state thermal energy harvesting system modeled through finite element analysis (FEA) in COMSOL.
- **BCI Real-Time Mobility System for Paralyzed Users**: EEG/EMG neuro-signal processing pipeline translating motor cortex impulses into real-time directional control for wheelchairs and assistive prosthetics.
