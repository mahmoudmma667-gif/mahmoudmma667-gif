## Research Statement & Philosophy | البيان البحثي والفلسفي

I am a **transdisciplinary researcher** operating at the intersections of **Human-Computer Interaction (HCI)**, **Human-Centered Technology (HCT)**, **Science, Technology, and Society (STS)**, **Computer Accessibility**, and **Educational Technology**.

My academic inquiry investigates how computational perception, spatial mathematics, and intelligent interfaces can be synthesized to eliminate physical barriers between human intent and digital execution. Rather than viewing technology as an autonomous artifact, my methodology treats technical architectures as deeply embedded within social contexts—prioritizing equitable access, pedagogical efficacy, cognitive ergonomics, and sensory-motor inclusivity.

> **الرؤية البحثية:**  
> باحث عابر للتخصصات يستكشف فلسفة وتطبيقات التكنولوجيا المتمحورة حول الإنسان (**HCT**)، وتفاعل الإنسان مع الحاسوب (**HCI**)، ودراسات العلوم والتكنولوجيا والمجتمع (**STS**). ينصب تركيزي الأكاديمي والتطبيقي على إمكانية الوصول الشامل للحاسوب (**Computer Accessibility**)، ونظم التعليم الرقمي المتقدمة (**Educational Technology**)، عبر تطويع خوارزميات الرؤية الحاسوبية والرياضيات المكانية لتطوير واجهات طبيعية تخدم الإنسان والمجتمع بأسره.

## Core Domains of Inquiry | مجالات البحث العلمي الأساسية

### 1. Human-Computer Interaction (HCI) & Human-Centered Technology (HCT)
- **Natural & Perceptual User Interfaces (NUI/PUI)**: Markerless camera-based hand tracking, multi-point kinematic analysis, and real-time gesture decomposition.
- **Latency & Temporal Ergonomics**: Mathematical optimization of the input-to-display loop, sub-millisecond signal filtering, and deterministic multi-threaded render pipelines.
- **Embodied Cognition & Spatial Computing**: Translating biological human gestures into precise three-dimensional geometric representations in Cartesian space.

### 2. Science, Technology, and Society (STS)
- **Democratization of Advanced Computing**: Developing zero-cost, hardware-agnostic interfaces running on ubiquitous consumer sensors (commodity webcams) rather than prohibitive proprietary hardware.
- **Socio-Technical Implications of AI**: Analyzing how neural network models reshape learning environments, labor workflows, and human agency.
- **Ethical & Epistemological Frameworks**: Ensuring computational systems respect cognitive autonomy, data sovereignty, and human dignity.

### 3. Computer Accessibility & Assistive Technologies (إمكانية الوصول للحاسوب)
- **Motor-Constrained Input Modalities**: Engineering adaptive spatial control interfaces that empower individuals with physical limitations to interact seamlessly with computing systems.
- **Universal Design Principles**: Implementing multi-modal feedback loops (spatial audio, visual cues, adaptive thresholding) to accommodate diverse physical and cognitive capabilities.
- **Barrier-Free CAD & Digital Manufacturing**: Translating gestural and natural language intent into watertight, physical 3D-printable geometry.

### 4. Educational Technology & Pedagogical Ergonomics (تكنولوجيا التعليم)
- **Interactive Spatial Pedagogy**: Utilizing real-time interactive simulations to elevate conceptual understanding in spatial mathematics, physics, and engineering.
- **Cognitive Load Optimization**: Designing lean, intuitive graphical user interfaces that reduce extraneous cognitive load during digital learning sessions.
- **Constructivist Digital Tools**: Building software frameworks where learners construct tangible digital knowledge structures through direct manipulation.

## Scientific & Methodological Competencies | المهارات والمنهجيات العلمية

### Computational & Mathematical Foundations
- **Computer Vision & Perceptual Kinematics**: Neural hand landmarker inference (MediaPipe), feature extraction (OpenCV), 3D coordinate normalization, Palm Scale Invariance metrics.
- **Spatial Geometry & Computational Topology**: Spline interpolation (Catmull-Rom), parametric surface synthesis, Constructive Solid Geometry (CSG Boolean operations), polygon tessellation, manifold verification.
- **Signal Processing & Noise Mitigation**: Low-pass temporal smoothing, adaptive velocity thresholding, multi-pass jitter absorption.

### System Architecture & Engineering
- **High-Throughput Runtime Design**: Multi-threaded execution models, asynchronous worker synchronization, WebAssembly (WASM) compilation, GPU delegate acceleration.
- **Modern Spatial & Web Graphics**: WebGL 2.0, Three.js scene graph manipulation, shader compilation, physically-based rendering (PBR) pipelines, client-side binary serialization (STL, GLB, Wavefront OBJ).
- **Core Languages & Toolchains**: Python, TypeScript, modern JavaScript, C++ fundamentals, Shell scripting, Git-centric version control.

### Academic & Research Rigor
- **Empirical Benchmarking**: Systematic latency quantification, frame-time variance tracking, spatial accuracy metrics, and reproducible test suites.
- **Open-Science & Software Reproducibility**: Research software documentation adhering to JOSS standards, comprehensive academic citation frameworks (BibTeX, CITATION.cff).
